Cyan is the only decorative accent, and it marks one thing per screen. Amber and red are never decoration — amber is the car's state, red is a refusal. If a colour is not carrying meaning it is a hairline.

Motion happens on arrival and on state change, never on idle. Nothing pulses, breathes or shimmers — a car app that animates while nothing is happening is lying about activity.
